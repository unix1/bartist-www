Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This policy covers the BARTist iOS app and this website, bartist.app.

In short

BARTist does not collect personal data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics.

The app

The BARTist app does not collect personal data. It does not use ads, tracking, or analytics.

To show live departure times, the app uses BART’s public API. BART’s privacy policy applies to that service.

The app is available on the App Store. Apple’s privacy policy applies to iOS and the App Store.

Because we do not collect data, we do not store, share, or sell any, and there is nothing for us to delete if you ask.

The website

This website does not use cookies, does not collect personal data, and does not use tracking or analytics.

Like most websites, it is hosted by a third-party provider.

Children

BARTist is not directed at children. We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version will always be on this page, with the date above.
